11+ English Prep

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 11+ English Prep handles information when you use the app.

Summary

11+ English Prep does not collect, sell, or share your personal information with the developer. The app does not require you to create an account, sign in, or submit personal details.

Information the app stores on your device

The app stores limited study state locally on your device so your progress can be restored when you reopen the app. This local app data may include:

  • Your selected study mode
  • Your selected test
  • Your sort preference
  • Your position within the word bank or a test

This information is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Data the app does not collect

11+ English Prep does not collect or transmit to the developer:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, photos, files, or messages
  • Payment card information
  • Advertising identifiers for ads or profiling
  • Analytics or crash reporting data through third-party SDKs

Speech feature

The app includes a text-to-speech feature to pronounce words. This feature uses text-to-speech services available on your device. The app itself does not send your personal information to the developer through this feature.

Internet and third parties

The app does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or social sign-in services. The app does not include its own internet-based data collection for the developer.

Children

The app is intended as an educational vocabulary tool. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Data security

Because the app stores only limited study progress locally on your device, you are responsible for the security of your device and any platform-level backup settings you enable.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
